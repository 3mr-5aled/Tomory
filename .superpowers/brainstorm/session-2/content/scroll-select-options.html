Lenis Momentum Scroll

A modern, lightweight library that provides smooth, inertial scrolling. It feels "buttery" and is standard for high-end React sites.

Total Unselectable

Applies user-select: none to the entire body. Users cannot highlight any text, protecting your content from easy copying.

Locked Content

Smart Unselectable

Locks text globally but keeps interactive elements (like inputs and admin panels) selectable to ensure the site remains usable.

Momentum Scroll Benefits

  • Premium, high-end "feel"
  • Consistent experience across browsers
  • Synchronizes with parallax animations

Unselectable Considerations

  • Prevents accidental highlighting
  • Can affect UX for users who like to copy text (e.g., product names)
  • Smart approach is recommended to keep inputs functional
